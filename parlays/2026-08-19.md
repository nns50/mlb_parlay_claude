### ⚠ BEFORE THE CARD: a tooling bug found this run changes what every number below means

`truep.py` stamps **`GLOBAL_SHRINK×0.5`** into the `[adj: …]` ledger tag whenever the governor is armed.
`pulse.py`, `calib.py` and `settle.py` all detected parlay **ticket** rows by testing for a literal `×`
in the leg cell. So from **8/12** — the day the shrink armed — **every governed leg was classified as a
parlay ticket and dropped from the governor's own window.** `clv_capture.py` had this exact fix applied on
8/16; it was never propagated to the other three.

Consequences, all verified after the fix:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `pulse.py` window | 96 decided legs | **127** |
| `calib.py` §1b scored legs | 250 | **280** |
| `adj:wind_out_over` (pulse) | 7-7 | **11-15** |
| GLOBAL SHRINK | **armed** since 8/12 | **does not fire** |

⚠ **The shrink was halving every adjustment on the strength of a subsample it had created by excluding the
shrunk legs.** It is not "wrong that we were cautious" — it is that the caution was an artifact, and eight
days of pricing ran at half magnitude for a reason that did not exist. Fixed in all three tools, with a
regression assertion added to `selftest.sh` (**115 checks, all passing**).
