### ⚠ METHODOLOGY DECLARATION — TrueP re-anchored to the current market baseline

**This changes published numbers, including reversing one Build B rejection, so it is stated up front
rather than slipped into a table.**

Doctrine defines **TrueP = market no-vig baseline + registered adjustments**, and `truep.py` takes
`--base-prob` from a *fresh* `devig.sh`. Build B instead held each leg's 11:00 TrueP **fixed** while
the price moved, so a leg that got more expensive showed a shrinking edge (that is how LAD fell from
+3.0pp to +2.4pp and lost its anchor eligibility). **Build C re-derives TrueP off the 18:00 baseline
on every leg.** The consequence is that a leg's edge stays pinned at its tag magnitude, and price
movement expresses itself in the **floor and the payout** instead of in a moving edge number.

**Why re-anchoring is the correct reading, per the 8/12 audit:** the audit's binding finding is that
*"a '+Npp edge' IS the adjustment — TrueP = market no-vig + tags, so the edge number and the tag are
the same object."* We hold exactly one +3 tag on Detroit. **Holding the 16:00 TrueP fixed would have
printed a +5.1pp edge on Detroit tonight — a number we know is impossible**, because no second tag
fired. That is the tell.

**And it cuts against us where it matters, which is why I trust it:** re-anchoring **cuts Detroit's
floor from 54.6% to 52.1%** (the flattering direction was the fixed one) while **rescuing PHI from
+2.7pp back to +3.0pp** and **restoring LAD's anchor eligibility that Build B removed**. A methodology
that only ever added legs would be suspect; this one takes the card's headline number down.

⚠ **What re-anchoring DISCARDS, and how it is handled.** Absorbing an adverse move into the baseline
means the edge never registers that *the market disagreed with us*. **That signal is not thrown away —
it is carried as an explicit counter-signal in prose** (see Detroit below) rather than as a phantom
edge adjustment. It is not re-priced twice.

⚠ **Baselines are now 11-book CONSENSUS, not best-of-both-sides.** Devigging the best price of each
side across different books manufactures a fake ~0.5% hold and flatters our edge — the exact artifact
the 8/12 audit measured. Consensus is the honest baseline; the **best price is still what we bet**.
This costs Detroit another 0.4pp (49.5% best-of-both vs **49.1% consensus**).
