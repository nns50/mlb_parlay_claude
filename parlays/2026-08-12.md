### ⛔ Two silent parser bugs found in `calib.py` and fixed today

This is the run's biggest finding and it is a measurement finding, not a betting one.

**Bug 1 — bolded `[adj: …]` tags were invisible.** `parse_adj_tags` matched a tag name starting at a
letter, but this ledger's house style bolds the magnitude: `[adj: **hitter_park_over+3 (full
magnitude)**]`. The leading `*` defeated the regex, so **every bolded tag row was silently filed under
`(none — market-anchored)`** in §1c and in `pulse.py`'s per-adjustment dimensions. Fix: strip `*` and
backticks before matching. What it changes:

| Adjustment | §1c as read yesterday | §1c after the fix |
|---|---|---|
| `hitter_park_over` | 11 rows, 6-5, skill **−0.0034** | **15 rows, 9-6, skill +0.0005** |
| `pitcher_park_under` | 7 rows, 3-4, skill **−0.0092** | **14 rows, 9-5, skill +0.0004** |
| `wind_out_over` | 1 row, n=1 | **15 rows, 9-6, skill +0.0032** |

**Bug 2 — an aggregate ledger row was eating a real leg's settled result.** `leg_key` took the first
over/under it found in a cell, so the row *"STRUCK BY DOCTRINE — TB @ ATH Over 8.5/10.5 **AND** MIL @
SD Under 7.5"* minted the key of a genuine single leg. Being the day's last row with that key, it
swallowed **TB @ ATH Over 8.5 — a 16-run WIN** out of the calibration bands, §1b Brier, §1c and the
governor's window entirely. Fix: two or more distinct lines in one cell now keys as `aggregate`.

Both are the silent-measurement class `selftest.sh` exists to catch, and **both now have assertions**
(`selftest.sh` 106 → 108 checks, all passing). The honest consequence is stated in `fades.md`: the
8/7 park-tag promotion was made on the **process bar (3 sightings)** and **stands on that basis** — it
is not being reversed on one day's re-read — but the "`calib.py` §1c corroborates" sentence attached
to it was reading a broken parser and is **withdrawn**. Carried to the n≥20 review.
